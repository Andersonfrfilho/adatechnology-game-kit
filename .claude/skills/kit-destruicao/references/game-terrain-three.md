# @adatechnology/game-terrain-three

three.js renderer for @adatechnology/game-terrain: chunked ground with a material pattern per surface, simplified far away, the marks as instanced decals, rebar where a slab is dug past its cover, and the clods.

API, uso e exemplos: `packages/game-terrain-three/README.md`; testes em `packages/game-terrain-three/tests/`.
