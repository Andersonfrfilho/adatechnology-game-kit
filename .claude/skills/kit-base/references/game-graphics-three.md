# @adatechnology/game-graphics-three

three.js adapter of @adatechnology/game-graphics: puts the player's graphics settings on a WebGLRenderer, camera and sun (pixel ratio, shadow map and box, far plane), names the GPU and times whole frames on it.

API, uso e exemplos: `packages/game-graphics-three/README.md`; testes em `packages/game-graphics-three/tests/`.
