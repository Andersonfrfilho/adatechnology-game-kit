# Game slice: stories

Generated by `pnpm --filter @adatechnology/characters-catalog-tool catalog:game` (spec 001, Phase 8, T8.2). **This folder goes to the zombiada repository**
(`content/stories/`): the valley's people, its 47 day-by-day events as `@adatechnology/game-stories` stories, the actions of the player, the life
simulation's tables and the two roles of the starting block. The engine (casting, stories, chronicle, sheet, life rules) is `game-stories`; the name
pools and the sheet ruleset are `game-characters-people`.

| File | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `cast.json` | The 45 people as `Person`: name, nickname, age, sex, personality, base, how they start (Arnaldo and Azevedo undead, Vitória unborn) | Os Vivos do Vale `NPCS`, `EXTRA`, `NICK`, `FEM`, `START_Z` |
| `stories.json` | The 47 events (`EV`) as 13 stories of beats with conditions, text pieces and changes, word for word; the golden `game-stories-life.json` checks them | Os Vivos do Vale `EV` (lines 372-497), converted in `tools/characters-catalog/src/slice-stories.ts` |
| `roles.json` | Seu Cícero and Sandra as roles: their kin (Rosa's brother, Jéssica's cousin), age and trade are the game's, their first names are drawn | jogo.js `NPC` (decision P9) |
| `actions.json` | The 13 player actions and their windows | Os Vivos do Vale `ACTS` |
| `life.json` | `LifeOptions` of the valley: the 18 bases, relations, couples, risk by place and by person, hostile bases, raiders, traitors' destinations, food drift, midwives, the ways in and the radio | Os Vivos do Vale `GROUPS`, `RELS`, `COUPLES`, `REG`, `EXPO`, `HOSTB`, `CAP`, `ROOM`, `life()` |

How the game uses it:

```ts
const world = createStoryWorld({ people: cast, stories: [...stories, roles], life, seed })
world.act('helena') // the player rescued Helena
world.runTo(90)
world.chronicle.entries
```
