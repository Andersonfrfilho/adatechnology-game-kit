The running film of the Fluid Standard (T5.3a, spec 001): `createFilm({ layout, profile, random })`, a literal port of the page's rules (F60-114). Drops land as impacts stretched by `1 / sin(angle)` with spikes, satellites and tails; a 180 Hz thin-film step limited to the wet box (at most 6 steps per frame, or `maxSteps`) runs rivulets with a bulb at the tip, follows the slope and the joints and dries from the thin edge in. Drips, continuous sources and drag smears are included. New: `PADRAO_LAYOUT`, `WORKSHOP_LAYOUT`, `createFoldedLayout`, `layoutFromSurfaces({ floor, wall?, curb?, center })` (the fold built from the host's `SurfaceSpec`s), `FILM_PROFILES` and `filmProfileOf` (`data/film-profiles.json`: blood, old blood, bile, motor oil, fuel, with the Workshop's `spikes` and `landsAsBlob`), `createFilmLcg`, the `FilmSimulator` interface (the CPU version is the reference for a later GPU one) and `film.setWeather({ rain })` (drying stops, spread x1.5; a no-op until `game-weather` drives it). The film never calls `Math.random`: the host gives the stream, so a LAN client with the same seed gets the same film. Additive: nothing existing changes.
