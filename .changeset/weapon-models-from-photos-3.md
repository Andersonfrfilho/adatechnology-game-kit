The launchers under the barrel are drawn from their photos instead of one generic tube: the M203 with its ribbed barrel sleeve, receiver and loop trigger guard; the GP-25 with its short muzzle-loaded barrel, body, quadrant sight and its own raked pistol grip; the AG36 with its long housing over the barrel, ladder sight, pistol grip and loop guard. Each clamps up to the handguard of its host, and the tube still hangs from the sliding pivot the reload clip moves. The AG36 reference photo is now one of the launcher on a G36.
