`ModuleHost.addObject` (game-contracts, implemented by game-test-room): a module puts an item of the host's catalogue in the world as scenery; it stays through clears and area openings, takes shots, blows, blasts and fire like any item, and the scenarios run without it. The weather visual's `rigItem` uses it: in the test room the headlights car is the fleet's real sedan (holes, glass, sparks, tyres, doors), no longer a drawing the shots went through.
