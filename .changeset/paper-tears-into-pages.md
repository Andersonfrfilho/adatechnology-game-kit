A shot book tears into loose pages instead of slabs as thick as the book. New `MaterialDef.sheet` (thickness of one sheet; paper 1 mm): a `tear` part thicker than a few sheets comes apart as layers of thin scraps, a little askew, three in four marked `Fragment.inner` (pages) and the rest cover. New `visual.innerColor` (paper: cream) colours the inner scraps and the fibres a hit throws out. The renderer lets those thin scraps glide and flutter down before they settle flat.
