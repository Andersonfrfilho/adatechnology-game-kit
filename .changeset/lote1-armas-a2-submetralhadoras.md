Three more real weapons in the arsenal, each with a model drawn after its photo and not borrowed from another weapon: MP40 (tubular receiver, magazine well, Bakelite housing and grip, ribbed barrel with the rest bar, stock that folds under), B&T MP9 (compact polymer block, magazine in the grip, vertical foregrip, top rail, stock that folds to the right) and FN PS90 (thumbhole stock with the trigger guard cut through the body, translucent magazine on top, integrated optic housing and the 407 mm barrel). Real numbers and sources in `tools/weapons-catalog/decisions.json` (`creations`); the models are `weaponModels` decisions with the new `mp40`, `mp9` and `ps90` builders. The FN P90 keeps its model.
