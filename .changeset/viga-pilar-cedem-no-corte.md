Reinforced concrete gives where it is shot through, not as a whole. A section shot away at a place where a lying member rests on its support (a bond beam on its wall) takes nothing from what it carries: its weight goes straight down there. A standing member carries through a cut only what rests on it and its own weight above the cut (a pillar cut under its top carries little there), and if the cut gives, what rested on the member stops bearing on it (the bond beam goes on resting on its wall and the other pillars) while the member stands, damaged, the concrete above the cut held by its cage. `sideOnOverpressure(tnt, distance)` gives the peak side-on overpressure of a blast (Kinney and Graham), and a sweep of charges and distances checks a confined wall against it and against the scaled distance of a local breach.
