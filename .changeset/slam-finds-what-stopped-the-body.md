A body thrown into something finds what stopped it: the slam's ray reaches the far side of the footprint just ahead (a steel sheet on its stand stopped the body 0.6 m from the panel, past the fixed 0.65 m reach by one walk step) and, when it misses at the shoulder or the hip, sweeps the footprint's height (the panel floats above the hip once the blast has taken its posts). Before, whether the slam named the wall depended on how much of the blast's destruction the frame budget had applied when the body arrived, so it changed with the machine and the Node version.
