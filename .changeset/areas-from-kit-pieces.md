The test areas use the pieces of the kit instead of boxes: the weapons counters are the buffet of the furniture catalogue, the shelves and the finds table of the items area are a workbench and a meeting table of the catalogue, the 1.80 m reference is a person the host gives (`person` of `createItemsTestModule`; `personSpecOf` of `game-characters-three` is exported for it), and the probe of the sound bench is what the host gives (`probe` of `createAudioTestModule`, a portable radio of the goods catalogue in the kit's room). Without `person` or `probe` the area draws none.
