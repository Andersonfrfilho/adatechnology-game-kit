The hands work the parts a reload clip moves (`data/hand-work.json`: slide rack or thumb release on a pistol, charging handle on ARs and AKs after an empty reload, bolt handle, loading port, crane, belt cover), following the clip's own timeline; `ToolClipPlayer.running` exposes the clip time. The right index finger rests along the frame, goes to the trigger after a shot and pulls with the trigger part (`HandTargets.fingers.index`).
