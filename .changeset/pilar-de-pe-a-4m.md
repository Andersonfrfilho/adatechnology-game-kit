A reinforced member (pillar, beam) further from a blast than its cut reach is no longer broken by the blast's generic damage: it keeps a quarter of its hit points and is not severed at one band, and weakens only where the wave knocks its concrete out block by block. 4.8 kg of TNT 4 m off a house wall (Z 2.37, about 140 kPa) used to break three pillars; now every pillar stands, damaged, and only a charge against a pillar cuts it.
