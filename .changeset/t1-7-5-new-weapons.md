21 new weapons (data from `decisions.creations`, each number with its source and reason): `hk_mp5sd3` (integral suppressor), `fn_parafal`, `taurus_t4_civil`, `taurus_th380`, `mossberg_500_cruiser`, `hk_mp5k`, `hk_ump9`, `mini_uzi`, `hk_mp7a1`, `hk_g36`, `hk_g36k`, `hk_g36c` (integral 3x optic on the first two), `fn_m249`, `fn_minimi_para`, `fn_mag`, `rpk`, `sig_mpx`, `mg3`, `pp19_bizon`, `vz61` and `m134`, and the calibres `4_6x30` and `32acp`. The M134 has three modes (hand-held with a belt box, with the ammunition backpack, on a tripod), a rate range (`rpmRange`, 2000 to 6000, 3000 by default) and a barrel spin-up (`spin`). The GP-25 fits only AK weapons (not the SKS). A factory part that is subsonic with its parts on (the MP5SD3) has the subsonic effect in its base numbers. `game-weapons-three` draws them (two new builders: the M134 rotary barrels that turn with each shot, and the Bizon helical magazine), keeps the 77 gallery hashes and has its own golden for the new models; the weapons area has four columns of counters and the M134 on its tripod.
