A reinforced member cast round its cage (a pillar or beam in concrete blocks) is cut through by a blast only at or near contact (`MEMBER_CUT_REACH`, half the breach reach); further off the blast takes the concrete it reaches out of it block by block and the cage holds it to what it rests on. The bare cage of a pillar keeps what its bars carry (`cageResidualCapacity`: perimeter bars by the steel's crushing strength, halved for buckling) instead of a flat 15%, so the end pillar of a wall stands on its cage under a light bond beam.
