Add `@adatechnology/game-vehicles`: a generic procedural vehicle engine for three.js (cars, trucks, buses, bikes, tanks, boats, planes and helicopters) with zone-based damage in five bands, cracking glass, particles, fluids, rollover, explosions and a damage-map UI. `createVehicleKit({ THREE, locale })` returns a per-instance kit; fleets, fuels, scenarios and texts come from plugins. Strings live in `locales/en.json` and `locales/pt-BR.json`. See MIGRATION.md for the old-to-new name table.
