Trip wire: `ExplosiveDef.tripwire` (`length`, `angleDeg`, `height`; default in `rules.explosives.tripwire`, 10 m ahead and 0.15 m above the charge, derived because no source gives the M18A1 kit length) is carried by the `place` use of a charge with the `wire` trigger (the Claymore), and `tripwireSegment(use)` gives the segment to stretch. The test room draws the wire (`charge_wire`), puts a marker (`charge_marker`) where any charge was set down and sets the charge off when a body (the player, an object of the room) crosses the wire; bodies already on the wire when it was set are let through until they step off. The Y key now fires only the charges for the remote detonator.
