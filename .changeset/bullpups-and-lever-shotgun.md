Four new weapons with models drawn from reference photos: FAMAS (carry handle over the whole top, grooved spigot, folding bipod legs), IWI Tavor (one olive polymer body, full-hand trigger guard, reflex sight on a short rail), L85A2 (steel body with SUSAT optic, tan quad-rail handguard) and Winchester 1887 (lever-action shotgun with tall receiver, big lever loop, wood stock and forend). New `look` option of the bullpup builder and `m1887` option of the rifle builder; the Steyr AUG and the other models are unchanged.
