New package: the three.js side of game-shapes. `toGeometry` (three's type, parameters and prototype), `shapeGeometries(THREE)` (three's geometry constructors made by game-shapes, bit-identical, for ported code), `toObject3D` (meshes per batch, part groups, InstancedMesh, LOD), `createMaterials` and `mergeByMaterial` on the core merge.
