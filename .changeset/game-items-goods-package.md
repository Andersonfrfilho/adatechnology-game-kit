New package: the real-world item catalog for `game-items`. 327 items with real size and weight, 17 categories, 24 places, 17 containers, recipes, use effects, house pools, depletion and spawn tables, ammo boxes, provenance and pt-BR (the team's names) and en texts. Generated by `tools/items-catalog`; `createGoodsItems()` and `goodsPack` plug it into `createItems`.
