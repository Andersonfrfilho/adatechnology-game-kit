Test table: `room.testTable` lays the catalogue on a `test_table` scenery piece a page at a time (with a search that finds the page of an item) and runs a trial on the item picked: a shot, a punch, a knife blow, a flamethrower burst or a fragmentation grenade set off beside it, plus a **Put back** that restores the table and the page. The room's panel gets a **Test table** card. `examples/test-room` uses the long dining table of the furniture catalogue (`?table=<group>` lays a group on start).
