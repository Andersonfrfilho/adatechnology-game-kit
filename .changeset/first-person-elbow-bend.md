First-person arms keep a visible elbow bend on a long gun: when a hand is out of reach the shoulder comes forward only until the elbow is bent by the new `FirstPersonRig.reachBend` (right 1.35 rad, left 0.95 rad by default), instead of until the arm is almost straight; `reachShift` goes from 0.35 to 0.42 m so the forend of a rifle stays in the support hand.
