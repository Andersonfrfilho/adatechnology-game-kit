The Heckler & Koch G36, G36K and G36C as the real ones (compared with the photos in `assets/weapon-references.json`): the tall polymer receiver with the stock hinge and the trigger group housing, the skeletal side-folding stock, the grip leaning back, the translucent magazine with the rounds seen through it, and on top the carry handle with its optic and red dot (G36, G36K) or the long rail on two risers ending in the front sight (G36C, with a rail under its short handguard). New `g36` model option and `stock: 'g36'`, `magMat: 'smoke'`; the options are decisions of the catalogue (`weaponModels` in `tools/weapons-catalog/decisions.json`). The goldens of the three models and their silhouettes are regenerated.
