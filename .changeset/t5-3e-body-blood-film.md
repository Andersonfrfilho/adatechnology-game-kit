The `body` events reach the running film of the liquids (T5.3e). `createFluidsBridge` takes a `film` sink (the `splat` of `createFilmBridge`): a `wound` throws 3 drops of blood, a `severed`, `destroyed` or `split` segment throws 4, 7 or 10 (options `spray`, `burstDrops`), thrown along the way out, in the colours of the body's `BloodLook` (`WoundBody.blood`). `bloodLookOfAge(age)` reads `ages.json` (fresh and dry colours, `blood` as the amount; ages thinner than 0.3 bleed `old_blood`), and the characters area takes `film` and `bloodOf(age)`. The test room exposes the film bridge as `room.film`.
