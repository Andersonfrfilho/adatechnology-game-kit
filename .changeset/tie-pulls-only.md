A `tie` joint (rope, cord, canvas lashing) now only bears load when the supported part hangs below the part it is tied to (a lamp from a ceiling). A rope to a stake in the ground, or to a pole beside the canvas, no longer holds anything up, so a tent whose poles are cut collapses even though its guy ropes and stakes are intact. Hold a canvas up with `resting` (or another pushing joint) on its poles, and keep the ropes as `tie`. `hinge` and `clip` are unchanged (they hold from the side).
