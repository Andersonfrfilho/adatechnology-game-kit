The outer baseboard of the houses (and of `wallPiece`) is a destructible band of glazed ceramic tiles instead of decoration: each band is a piece of its own (`baseboard:<wall>:<side>`, `ceramic`, the mass of 12 mm of tile and adhesive), laid in 0.6 m tiles as its units (`BASEBOARD_TILES`) and glued to its wall. A shot craters the tile where it lands (and the round goes on into the wall), a kick or a nearby blast cracks the glaze with the ceramic's own sound, a charge against it shatters it, and it comes off when its wall goes.
