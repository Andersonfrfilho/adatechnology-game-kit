The minigun is carried at the hip, by its handles: a model can name where its hands go (`holdRight`, `holdLeft`) and how the body carries it (`carry: 'hip'`). The M134 names its right spade grip and the carry handle on top; in first person it is held low and to the right (`VIEW_HOLD.hip`, no sights to bring to the eye), in third person at the right hip, outside the body (`BODY_HOLD.hipGrip`). Every other weapon is held as before.
