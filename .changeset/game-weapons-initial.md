Initial release (0.1.0): 18 real calibres and 45 generic weapon archetypes as data (firearms, melee, flame, heat, throwables, tools), a deterministic `WeaponState` runtime (magazines, fire modes, reload, spread and bloom, pellets, recoil, fuel) that produces `Shot`s and `Use`s, and an `en` / `pt-BR` locale table.
