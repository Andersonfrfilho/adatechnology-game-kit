Slash (blade) damage now follows the part's cross-section instead of its whole mass. A cut only goes through a slice of the part, so on a part longer than 1 m slash damage is multiplied by its longest dimension in metres (at most 50): a 6.8 m trunk costs as much to cut as a 1 m log of the same diameter, and cutting effort grows with the section area. Parts up to 1 m long are unchanged. A long trunk no longer takes thousands of axe blows. Blunt, ballistic, explosive and fire damage and joint wear are unchanged. Hosts that tuned slash damage on parts longer than 1 m (trunks, beams, poles) may need to retune.
