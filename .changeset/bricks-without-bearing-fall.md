'@adatechnology/game-destruction': minor
---

Bricks left in the air come down: after every unit a shot, a blow, a breach or a blast takes out of a wall of laid units, the units with no bearing down to the bottom of the wall (`unsupportedCells`: an island over the edge of a hole, a strip between two slots, a unit held by less than a quarter of its bed) fall as debris in one `chip` with `units`, leave the part (rays and shots pass where they were) and take their marks with them. A unit between two standing units of its course is wedged by its head joints, so a gap of one or two bricks holds.
