A burning part's flame is shown only while something of the part is drawn. A part with no mesh of its own (the rafters and ridge of a gable roof, which the house does not draw) or one that broke or burnt away no longer leaves its fire hanging in the air at its spec position; the fire comes back if the part is drawn again. Which parts ignite, fire spread, burn rates and durations are unchanged.
