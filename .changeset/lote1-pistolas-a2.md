Four weapons of the first lot, part A2, drawn as their photos instead of inheriting another weapon's model: the SIG Sauer P320, HK USP and Walther P99 (new `design` option of the pistol builder: modular or polymer frame with a grip of its own, serrated slides, rails, squared or hooked trigger guard, the USP's external hammer, the P99's strap ridges and dimples) and the Colt Python (new `python` option of the revolver builder: ventilated rib, full-length underlug, walnut checkered grip with medallion, round trigger guard). Existing pistols and revolvers are unchanged; the four are added to the catalogue (creations and model decisions), with goldens, thumbnails and docs.
