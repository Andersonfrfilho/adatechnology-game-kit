The body seen from outside closes its fingers around any tool that asks for a hand (`HandTargets.grip`, `gripL`): a knife, a sword held with both hands or a crowbar no longer sit in an open palm (only a firearm closed the fingers before). The second hand of a two-hand handle (the katana) closes as the first one does, and the brass knuckles are gripped with the rings ahead of the palm (`handle` option of the weapon item registry).
