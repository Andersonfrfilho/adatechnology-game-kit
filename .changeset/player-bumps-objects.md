Physics of the objects in the room (first object audited: the trash bin). The player no longer walks through spawned objects: what stands taller than a step blocks the walk, and the walk pushes what is light enough with the force of a person against the floor's grip (a 3 kg bin slides or tips, a 70 kg fridge only blocks); the walk moves in short steps so a long step cannot jump over a thin obstacle. A kick leaves the hip instead of the eye, so it reaches what is in front of the feet. A round through a ductile shell (plastic, sheet metal) leaves in it only the energy it took to cross it (two holes, the part does not burst); a blade with more energy than a thin wall takes goes through it (a knife into a plastic bin or a can). A burst of fragments over the live budget no longer builds the excess meshes only to fade them.
