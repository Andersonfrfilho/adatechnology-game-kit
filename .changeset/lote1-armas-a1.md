Six more real weapons in the arsenal, each with its model, reload and real numbers (the source of each in `tools/weapons-catalog/decisions.json`, `creations`): AKS-74U, FN SCAR-L, M1 Garand, Accuracy L96A1, Mossberg 590 and FN M240B. A generic weapon now wears the model of a weapon of its own before a variant of another (`variantOf`), so a weapon added later never changes what the generic looks like.
