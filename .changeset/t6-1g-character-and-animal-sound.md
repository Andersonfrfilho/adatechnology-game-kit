The voices and the animal calls on `game-audio` (T6.1g). `game-characters-three` gets the sound bank of the bodies, 16 recipes whose traces equal the team code (the game's `grito` and `voz`, the art scene's `voice` behind groan, scream and leap, plus inhale, grab, bite, hurt, flesh, sever and gore), the table of the voices of the 13 undead types (`UNDEAD_VOICES`) and `createCharacterSound(host.audio)` (voices by moment, `body` events, what the player hears, the screamer's wind-up). `game-animals-three` gets the four calls of the game's `bichoSom` and `createAnimalSound` with the page's cooldowns. Both test areas play on the room's engine (new actions `voice`, `special_call` and `call`). `game-audio` exports the art scene's primitives (`sceneNoise`, `sceneTone`, `sceneVoice`, `sceneRecipe`); `game-destruction-three` uses them instead of its own copy (same recipes).
