Strikes stop at contact: the update state of the first-person arms takes an optional `contact(origin, direction, maxDistance)` (distance to the first surface or null) that the room answers with its raycast, leaving out the player, the held item and the viewmodel. The hands and the kick foot stay a few millimetres in front of the surface along the camera forward, so the elbow bends instead of the hand entering the mesh.
