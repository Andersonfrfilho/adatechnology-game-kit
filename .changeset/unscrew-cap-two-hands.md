A screw cap is turned off by the other hand before the first bite or sip, in first and third person. `twistStroke` (game-contracts) is the hand turning a part round in strokes, for any cap, tap or knob; `ModuleItemSeal.openUse` and `turn` let a module open its seal with a use; the `unscrew` use (game-fluids) turns during its `turn` keys and opens at its `open` key; `turnItem` (game-items-three) turns and lifts the cap a model marks with `asLid`; the body takes the other hand's grip from `AvatarItemUsePose.gripL`.
