Each material answers each kind of blow its own way. New `MaterialDef.hits` (material × damage type): what a blow tears out where it lands (`tear`: `splinters`, `lumps`, `none`) and the joules per piece. A round into wood tears splinters of fresh wood out of the exit (and back out of the entry), MDF crumbles lumps, plywood splinters; a punch crumbles MDF, a kick splinters pine, hardwood and steel tear nothing. Snapping boards (MDF, plastic) also break where they are shot. `innerColor` for wood, plywood and MDF. Cases with shelves (`furnitureSpec`) are MDF.

Loose pieces land on what is under them: on other pieces and on the drawn meshes of fixed objects (`PieceSystemOptions.staticTopAt`), so a falling shelf rests on the books below and a tipping panel on the chair in front. New `DestructionRenderer.solidsNear` gives the drawn boxes around a point. The test room's player walks by them: steps onto low debris (up to 45 cm), stops at taller things and kicks light pieces (books, boards) out of the way, instead of the scenery's old footprints.
