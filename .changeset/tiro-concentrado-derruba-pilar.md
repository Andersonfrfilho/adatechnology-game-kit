Concentrated fire brings a reinforced member down where it is shot. Each round on a pillar or a beam takes a cone of concrete out of the band (10 cm along it) it lands on: as deep as the round goes into concrete (its `penetration.concrete`, or its energy over the material's resistance) and 3.4 calibres across. A crater deeper than the 3 cm cover bares the bars running through it. At 90 % of the band's section the member is cut there: the band comes out, on a standing member the concrete above the cut falls (its bars stay), and what rested on it no longer bears on it. On a 15 cm pillar that is 20 to 30 rifle rounds or 5 to 10 rounds of .50 on the same place; a pistol or a shotgun practically never.
