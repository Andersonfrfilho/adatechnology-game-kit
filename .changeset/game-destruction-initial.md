Initial release (0.1.0): engine-agnostic, deterministic destruction model. Materials as data decide how parts dent, get holes, crack, shatter, splinter, crumble, tear, burn, melt and come apart at their joints; ballistic, blunt, slash, explosive, fire and heat damage; tool-based disassembly; fluid leaks; serializable world; `en` / `pt-BR` locale table.
