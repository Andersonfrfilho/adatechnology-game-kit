The infill of a confined wall comes down as a panel under a blast that loads it whole: the reflected peak pressure and the reflected impulse over the bay the charge faces (between the two pillars around it) are read against a pressure-impulse curve of unreinforced brick (`(P - 70 kPa)(I - 150 Pa·s) >= 6e6`, at four of five points of the bay). 4.8 kg of TNT 4 m off brings the panel down while the pillars and the bond beam stand; a hand grenade a metre or more off, or any charge past Z 3.5, only cracks it.
