Twelve new types for rural homes and camping: `bunk_bed`, `wood_stove`, `fireplace` (brick or stone), `outhouse`, `well` (bucket, crank and rope parts, a `hose` fixture on a 20 000 l tank), `cooler` (loot table `cooler`), `campfire`, `camp_stove`, `gas_canister`, `lantern_lamp`, `camp_chair` and `camp_table`. New `rural` and `camp` groups, `item.flueHeight` / `item.chimneyHeight`, region data `canister`, items `ice_pack` and `sandwich`, locale keys in `en` and `pt-BR`, destruction tags on every body and part.

Adds `service_entrance` `variant: 'gato'`: an illegal tap with a bare drop wire and no pole, riser or meter box.
