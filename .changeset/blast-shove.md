An explosion shoves what is around it. `blastShove` (game-contracts) is the one rule, `min(maxSpeed, sqrt(2 * energy * coupling / mass)) * (1 - d / r)^2`, with every number in `BlastShoveOptions` (the room's `blastShove` option, lent to modules as `ModuleHost.blastShove`). The destruction renderer uses it for its pieces and, with `shoveable`/`onLoosened`, makes a heavy object that is not fixed a piece when a blast reaches it. The test room marks heavy, free items that way, and wraps an item whose builder scales the root so that its spec, box and mass are in metres (a can of beans was 0.7 m and 460 kg). The characters test area pushes its people and knocks them down. The explosives area has scenarios that measure the push of a P13 shot and a thrown M67.
