The default walk cadence range of the personality is 0.85 to 1.15 (it was 0.88 to 1.12), so a group of nine neighbours differs in step by a coefficient of variation of 0.056. A crowd with `personality` on and no explicit `cadence` walks differently from before; pass `cadence: [0.88, 1.12]` for the old numbers.
