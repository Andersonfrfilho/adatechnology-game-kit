A shot bookcase comes apart like one. A long wooden part (a board, a batten, a beam) breaks where it is hit: its length is cut in 10 cm bands with their share of its hit points, and the band that runs out breaks it across there, a stub each side and splinters between; a thin board goes in a few shots, a beam or a log takes many. New `structure.maxSpan`: a part whose remaining supports leave it a longer span falls with what rests on it. `furnitureSpec` stands shelves and racks as a carcass (the sides carry, each shelf needs both ends, the back carries nothing), so a broken side drops its shelves and their books. The test room's scenery takes `free`: shots whose push moves its mass (a shotgun, not a pistol) shove it. A light collapse of furniture raises a puff of dust instead of a storey's cloud.
